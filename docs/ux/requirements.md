# Interface requirements

Status: **the owner's current vision for the interface, adopted 2026-10-18.** It will change; it
changes only through a pull request that edits this file and records the owner decision (date and
link). Agents do not reopen a settled item in an issue, a mockup or a review: they cite its ID.

## How to use this file

- **Before interface work.** Interface agents read this file with
  [packages/ui/CATALOGUE.md](../../packages/ui/CATALOGUE.md) and [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).
  An item here is a requirement, not a suggestion.
- **Acceptance grid.** Every mockup round and every interface pull request is checked against the
  L-items, rendered and clicked, in FR and EN, navigation A (L101), at 393 px /
  100 % and 360 px / 130 %. The review reports PASS, PARTIAL, FAIL or NOT-TESTABLE per affected
  item, with evidence.
- **Open choices (O-items) are the owner's.** A proposal may show options side by side; it never
  settles one silently. When the owner decides, the item moves to the L-list in the same pull
  request.
- **Precedence.** Contracts (`docs/contracts/`) win on data and calculation semantics, and ADRs on
  architecture. On presentation and interaction, this file wins over `DESIGN.md` where they
  differ, until `DESIGN.md` is reconciled (see "Conflicts" at the end). A new owner decision that
  contradicts an item here replaces it through a pull request to this file.
- **Source links** abbreviate `https://github.com/Aristotelesqc/Flourprint` as `GH`
  (`GH/issues/111#issuecomment-…`). The items were compiled from issues #120 to #134, the issues
  they cite, `DESIGN.md`, the contracts and `AGENTS.md`.

## How to read the "Kind" column

Every comment and issue body read in scope is posted under the `Aristotelesqc` login, but **none
of them is verbatim owner prose**: each one is signed by an agent ("Generated by Claude Code",
Antigravity/Gemini, Codex, GPT-6) or is written in the third person about the owner. So no item
qualifies as an explicit, owner-written decision. The kinds are:

- **OD**: an owner decision given directly to an agent, dated, with the owner's words quoted.
- **OD-R**: recorded owner decision. An agent comment or body explicitly attributes it to the owner
  ("Owner decision", "Owner note", "Owner clarification", "_Owner: …_", "owner approved").
- **OI**: owner-filed issue requirement. It appears in the scope or acceptance of an issue the owner
  opened (#112, #120–#131, #100, #104), drafted by an agent from owner directions. It is binding
  acceptance, but it is not attributed to the owner word for word.
- **CL**: a finding on the owner-tracked #134 checklist. The owner adopted the checklist to carry
  findings into the next rounds ("The owner's decision on each point is recorded there").
- **C**: a canonical repo rule (contract, AGENTS.md or DESIGN.md). Where the text cites an owner
  decision, that is noted.
- **OPEN**: an open owner choice. Proposals must not settle it silently (listed at the end).

Screens: global, library (incl. template chooser), recipe (reading), edit, steps (incl. Timeline),
planning, preparation, history, registry/settings, plus a "mockup process" group for owner
requirements on how UI proposals are delivered.

## 1. Global: vocabulary and language

**L1.** French UI text never addresses the user: no "tu", no "vous". Instructions and actions use the infinitive ("Pétrir 8 min à basse vitesse"). Explanations use an impersonal voice. Screens: global. Source: AGENTS.md "Rules"; #30 body, "Additions of 2026-10-11 (owner decisions)" (GH/issues/30); GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6110744754 ("Owner preferences already decided"). Kind: C + OD-R.

**L2.** No user-facing string is hard-coded in components. Every text has `fr` and `en` message keys (ADR 0009). Screens: global. Source: AGENTS.md "Rules"; GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6110744754. Kind: C + OD-R.

**L3.** One locale per screen:

- no English words inside FR screens ("Live", "Snapshot figé", "⏱ 1 active");
- no French leftovers in EN (filters, statuses, durations, template sheet);
- EN numbers use EN separators ("6.4%", not "6,4 %"), the same in chips and rows.

Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/134 body, "Language mixed inside one locale"; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6219501036. Kind: CL.

**L4.** The execution session is named **Préparation** (singular) / **Preparation**. This sits beside Recette / Planification. The running-work destination or session must not be called "Fournée(s)" / "Bakes" / "Fournée active". "Fournée" is reserved for counting actual oven batches. Persisted names (`bake*`) are unchanged. Screens: global, preparation, history, navigation. Source: #30 body, "Decisions of 2026-10-12 (owner)" (GH/issues/30); GH/issues/30#issuecomment-6116627434; GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6110020897; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6219501036 (B's "Fournées / Bakes" rejected); GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6227770682; docs/contracts/bake-session.md "Preparation and persisted names". Kind: OD-R + C.

**L5.** **Cuire / Cook** is the generic cooking action. **Enfourner / Bake** is only a contextual label on an oven step. Use contextual per-method step actions (Pocher, Cuire au four, Cuire à la vapeur, Cuire à la poêle), never one global button that completes the whole cooking sequence. **Proof** is a fermentation environment, not a cooking outcome. Screens: global, preparation, steps. Source: GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6110020897 ("Owner clarification — general cooking, not oven-only baking"); #35 body ("Owner terminology clarification: Cuire / Cook", "Mixed-appliance cooking"); #30 body, "Record of 2026-10-11". Kind: OD-R.

**L6.** A preparation does not always end in baking. Valid results:

- `cooked`;
- `dough`, with disposition frozen, refrigerated, given, sold or other, and an optional piece count;
- `cookedAndDough`.

A kept, frozen, given or sold dough is _finished_, never _abandoned_. Neither the UI nor the
mockups may assume an oven or require cooking. Completing a cooking stage, even the last, never
ends the session, never sets an outcome and never means the product is done. Screens: global,
preparation, history, recipe. Source: docs/contracts/bake-session.md "Lifecycle", "Cooking stages",
"Outcome"; #35 body, "Behavioral UX and neutral preparation exploration"; GH/issues/30#issuecomment-6110005183. Kind: C + OD-R.

**L7.** The intended outcome is authored in Recipe and labelled as _planned_ ("Résultat prévu", not "Résultat : Cuit"). It stays distinct from the recorded outcome, which History shows. Finish defaults to the intended outcome and offers a choice beside the button, so finishing never forces a question. A legacy session with no outcome reads **unknown**, never "cooked". Screens: recipe, preparation, history. Source: GH/issues/121 acceptance 4; GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6190165747 item 11; docs/contracts/bake-session.md "Outcome"; docs/contracts/process.md "Model" (`intendedOutcome`). Kind: OI + C.

**L8.** "Démarrer" starts a Preparation **now**: timers run from the tap. The planner offers "Maintenant" or a chosen time. Planned time and actual start stay distinct. Screens: recipe, planning, preparation. Source: #30 body, "Additions of 2026-10-11"; GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6110744754; DESIGN.md "Layout". Kind: OD-R.

**L9.** Wording preferences:

- the quantity column is "Poids", not "Grammes";
- a plain "Retirer", not "Retirer tangzhong";
- "cuisson" is only the act of cooking (generalized to Cuire per method).

Screens: recipe, edit. Source: GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6110744754 ("Owner preferences already decided (preserve them)"). Kind: OD-R.

**L10.** The label **Hydratation / Hydration** means `totalWaterPct`: total water, counting every water-bearing ingredient. It is never "conventional hydration". There is no separate "eau libre" / "added water" / "poured water" summary line. Free water is one ingredient among the others, and a recipe may have none (milk shokupan). Screens: recipe, edit, global. Source: docs/contracts/formula.md (owner decisions of 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-12); GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6110744754; GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6135427798. Kind: C (owner decision) + OD-R.

**L11.** The hydration breakdown trigger and sheet read "Détail de l'hydratation" / "Hydration breakdown" (or "Contributions en eau"), never "D'où vient l'eau" / "Where the water comes from". Screens: recipe. Source: GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6205955164 ("Owner UX Feedback Addendum #3", recorded by AGY). Kind: OD-R.

**L12.** Every percentage names its base: "% de la farine" (of total flour) versus "% du boulanger" (baker's percentage), including the component allocation context. Screens: recipe, edit, global. Source: docs/contracts/formula.md "Presentation (owner decision of 2026-10-12)"; GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6137393718 (approved v2 corrections); GH/issues/120 "Accepted owner directions" 3. Kind: C (owner decision) + OD-R.

**L13.** No internals leak into the UI:

- no "Le plus tard possible" (`alap`);
- no raw graph edges as instructions ("Après X" is omitted when the step depends only on the one just before it);
- no raw component ids: use display names ("Tangzhong", not `tangzhong`);
- no unlabelled endpoint or readiness jargon: translate it into a baker's question, with the model details on demand;
- no orphan dot for an empty instruction.

Screens: steps, planning, recipe, global. Source: GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6190165747 items 8–9; DESIGN.md "Approved screen composition"; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6226163455; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6227305417; GH/issues/126 acceptance 3. Kind: CL + C + OI.

**L14.** No issue numbers, reviewer notes, "simulate" toggles or diagnostic-fixture controls inside the simulated product screens. They belong outside the phone frame. Screens: global (mockups). Source: GH/issues/134 body, "No issue numbers or reviewer notes inside the product screens"; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6227770682 item 5. Kind: CL.

## 2. Global: data integrity and numbers

**L15.** Unknown is never shown as 0, and no zero is fabricated:

- an unknown composition, quantity, duration or timing reads unknown or provisional, with its reasons;
- an unsupported or out-of-domain fermentation result is never "0 min–0 min";
- a duplicated model-driven step reads "duration required" and makes later steps `unknownAfter`;
- unknown group windows are never zero-filled.

Screens: global, recipe, steps, planning, preparation. Source: GH/issues/120 acceptance ("unknown timing is not zero-filled"); GH/issues/122 acceptance 3; GH/issues/123 acceptance 4; GH/issues/126 acceptance 1; docs/contracts/process.md "Relative timeline and groups", "Duplicate"; docs/contracts/formula.md (unknown never 0); DESIGN.md (fermentation card). Kind: OI + C.

**L16.** A partial known sum is not an estimate of the whole. An incomplete hydration, fat or sugar value is shown as a lower bound or known part ("≥ x %", "x % connu"), never with "≈". Complete, estimated and partial values are visibly distinct. One consolidated notice explains the incompleteness, with the contributing ingredients on demand. An approximate sign alone is not enough. Screens: recipe, library, global. Source: GH/issues/134 body, "'≈ 73 %' on a partial sum" (_Owner: to fix_); GH/issues/120 "Open decisions… Data integrity"; GH/issues/125 acceptance 2; GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6190165747 item 6. Kind: OD-R + OI.

**L17.** Known-zero noise may be hidden _in presentation_, but its contribution is never removed from the engine:

- 0 g of water from salt or sugar in the hydration breakdown;
- "Eau 0 g" in a weighing view.

Editing can still represent zero. A "(connue)/(known)" qualifier appears only for non-default statuses (estimated with range, unknown). Screens: recipe, edit, preparation. Source: GH/issues/120 "Data integrity"; GH/issues/122 acceptance 3; GH/issues/125 acceptance 4; GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6205955164. Kind: OI + OD-R.

**L18.** Numbers reconcile across every screen: library card, recipe, hydration sheet, Planning, Preparation and step counts ("Étape n / N" equals the real number of steps). They come from `@flourprint/core`, with no baking math in the UI. Values such as tangzhong evaporation are never invented unless the engine supplies them. Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/134 body, "Numbers that must reconcile"; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6227770682 item 1; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6228651482 ("Data and behavior come from round 3"); AGENTS.md "No baking math outside packages/core/domain". Kind: CL + OD-R + C.

**L19.** Rounding and precision:

- the Hydration summary is a whole percent while the engine keeps full precision;
- precise editing keeps the exact values;
- rounded display values never silently become edits;
- summary percentages use one consistent rounding (no 2-decimal fat/sugar beside a whole-percent hydration);
- component percentages have at most one decimal;
- each column of a list follows one rounding rule;
- the hydration sheet's precision matches the summary.

Screens: recipe, edit, global. Source: docs/contracts/formula.md "Presentation (owner decision of 2026-10-12)"; GH/issues/125 acceptance 1; GH/issues/121 acceptance 3; GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6190668727; GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6206059271; GH/issues/134 body, "Precision is consistent within a list". Kind: C (owner decision) + OI + OD-R + CL.

**L20.** Planned, estimated, observed and actual values are visibly distinct. A planned duration is never shown as elapsed or observed time. Planned elapsed time never implies biological readiness or a cooked result. Screens: preparation, history, steps. Source: DESIGN.md "Do's and Don'ts", "Completed preparation steps"; GH/issues/124 acceptance 5. Kind: C + OI.

**L21.** Fermentation display:

- no interface shows a volume or a "doubled" target (e.g. "Pâte doublée", "doublement franc du volume");
- progress is a percentage of the planned fermentation, with its target labelled "provisional";
- an unbounded band reads "not reached within 21 days", never "no limit";
- an extrapolated estimate always shows its reasons beside it.

Screens: recipe, preparation. Source: docs/contracts/fermentation.md (status: owner decision 2026-10-09), "Results"; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6227770682 item 2. Kind: C.

## 3. Global: look, layout, accessibility, motion, navigation

**L22.** Keep the warm orange/brown identity in Light, Warm Dark and AMOLED. AMOLED is a true black (#000) page with warm accents. All three are selectable, plus System, and checked as distinct themes. No generic cool or purple palette. Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/35#issuecomment-6109745942; #35 body, "Owner preference: preserve the warm baking palette"; DESIGN.md "Colors"; GH/issues/120 direction 6. Kind: OD-R + C.

**L23.** The logo (loaf with three scores under a dimension line) is final. It is not redrawn without the owner. Removing the app mark from the bar is only a preference to evaluate in #112. Screens: global, library. Source: GH/issues/30#issuecomment-6116627434; GH/issues/120 direction 7; GH/issues/131 "Owner option". Kind: OD-R.

**L24.** The UI is phone-only. There are no tablet or desktop responsive layouts. Desktop browsers and shells render a 1:1 phone frame of 412×915. Layouts must still work on real 360 and 393 px phones with larger text. Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/118; GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6205731046 §7 ("Per owner clarification"); GH/issues/120 direction 6. Kind: OD-R.

**L25.** Required coverage: 360 and 393 px, FR and EN, larger text (125–130 %) and Light/Dark/AMOLED. At 360 px with 125–130 % text:

- no mid-word breaks (no `overflow-wrap:anywhere` on labels);
- titles, including the recipe name in B's edit bar, are not truncated ("Shoku…", "Fournée …");
- pills ("+00:00 → +00:10", "Étape 7 / 7") do not wrap inside their rounded background;
- tabs do not wrap unevenly ("Historique (3)");
- ingredient names are not squeezed onto 2–3 lines (move or shrink the % column);
- icon-only fallbacks keep accessible names and need owner agreement.

Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/120 acceptance (navigation/a11y item); GH/issues/134 body, "Layout at 360 px with 125 % text"; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6226169253 P0-3; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6228651482. Kind: OI + CL + OD-R.

**L26.** Use rem units (an owner request recorded in DESIGN.md). Hit targets are 48 px (3rem). DESIGN.md records current conventions and _is not a rule_ (owner), so a size change goes through an owner choice and a DESIGN.md update. Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/134 body, "Do not claim implementation" (_rem units are the owner's request_) and "Arrow size" (_Owner: DESIGN.md records the current conventions; it is not a rule_); DESIGN.md "Layout". Kind: OD-R.

**L27.** Accessibility:

- every control is keyboard reachable (no `tabIndex -1` checkboxes);
- overlays move focus in, contain it and return it on close;
- Escape and an outside tap close menus and sheets;
- closed overlays are inert and hidden from assistive technology;
- disclosures expose `aria-expanded`;
- icon-only buttons have accessible names;
- reordering is announced;
- Android Back, keyboard and focus are checked where affected.

Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/120 acceptance (navigation, screen-reader/focus/keyboard); GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6219501036 §5; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6227770682; GH/issues/129 acceptance 2; GH/issues/123 acceptance 6. Kind: OI + CL.

**L28.** Motion is required ("UI animations must not be forgotten"). Round 2's motion inventory is kept, and may be improved but not dropped:

- tokens of 150, 220 and 260 ms with the M3 standard and decelerate curves;
- press, anchored-menu, row, checkbox, chevron, accordion, sheet and backdrop animations;
- tab or screen changes and step completion.

Every motion has a reduced-motion version (OS and manual). Motion never rolls numbers, never
simulates progress and never delays an action. Each transition has a stated purpose, and no action
waits for an animation to finish. Screens: global. Source: GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6228656575 ("Owner addition, 2026-10-17"); GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6228670659 ("Round 2 motion must be kept (owner)"); DESIGN.md "Motion direction"; GH/issues/130 acceptance. Kind: OD-R + OI + C.

- rows are compact;
- Trash is separated from the other actions;
- Duplicate is present;
- no action duplicates one already in the bar ("Modifier sur place").

Sheets open from the bottom of the **viewport**, not the bottom of the content. Screens: global, recipe. Source: GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6226235543 ("Owner decisions (2026-10-16)"); GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6228651482 (round 2 anchored menu); GH/issues/111#issuecomment-6205813397 §1 ("following a discussion with the owner"); GH/issues/134 body, "Global destination hidden…"; GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6228380029. Source: GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6228651482 ("delete versus trash" stays on the checklist); GH/issues/134#issuecomment-6227770682 item 4. Kind: OD-R.

**L31.** No persistent full-width bottom call-to-action ("Démarrer/Lancer la préparation", "+ Nouvelle recette"). Each screen's actions sit in a thumb-reachable bar, and Start lives in that bar. **L101.** Navigation is **A**, with the section tabs at the top (owner decision, 2026-10-19, which
settles O1; navigation B and its bottom bar of large destination buttons are no longer developed):

- each screen keeps A's contextual bottom toolbar (L31, L32, L60);
- the tabs at the top stay tappable, and a horizontal swipe also moves to the next or previous
  tab;
- both gestures change the tab: a slow drag past half the width, or a short quick flick above a
  velocity threshold, whatever the distance;
- the current screen follows the finger and the next one slides in like a carousel; on release
  the motion finishes or springs back at a speed matched to the gesture, and the tab indicator
  moves with it;
- a swipe starts only on a clearly horizontal gesture; vertical scrolling wins otherwise;
- no swipe starts near the screen edges (Android Back gesture), during a step drag (L96), or
  inside an area that already scrolls horizontally;
- with reduced motion the tab changes at once, without the slide;
- switching tabs while editing keeps the same draft (L33).

The motion is a library item (`fp.motion.*`) shown in the catalogue. The owner wants it truly
fluid. Polishing the other animations is deferred, possibly to 0.4; L28 still applies. Screens:
global, recipe. Source: owner, 2026-10-19 ("je veux A avec les tabs au haut", "on devrait aussi
pouvoir swiper à gauche ou à droite", "un petit flick rapide horizontal du doigt"). Kind: OD.

**L145.** Every fermentation forecast is presented as an estimate, and the baker's own judgement comes first:

- forecast end times, remaining times, progress, dough temperature and self-heating are labelled as estimates wherever they appear. They are never phrased as the dough being ready. The information on demand says what they rest on: model, assumed temperatures, container;
- on a fermentation step, the baker's sensory readiness criteria lead and the estimate follows. The criteria are rise against the baker's own reference (for example a mark on the container), the poke test, bubbles on the surface and sides, jiggle and feel;
- a recorded "dough ready" (or "not ready yet") observation decides for that step and overrides the model. The interface then follows the observation, not the computed end;
- like a weather forecast, a forecast time is shown as a likely time with a range (for example "ready around 14:30, between 14:00 and 15:15"), never as a single to-the-minute time. The range comes from the model's envelope. It is wider when the container or conditions are unknown, and narrower once the baker's own readings have calibrated the personal factor;
- as the forecast end approaches, the interface invites the baker to check the dough physically, before any reminder presents the step as due;
- uncertainty accumulates along the process. Earlier transitional fermentation steps (for example a bulk ferment at room temperature or in the fridge) may run on fixed planned durations. The last fermentation step before baking carries the accumulated uncertainty of all earlier steps and is the least certain. That step shows the widest range and puts the physical checks (poke test first) ahead of the estimate. When they disagree with the model, the physical cues prevail;
- extends L20 and L21: L21's ban on a model-computed volume or "doubled" target still holds, while a rise the baker observes against their own reference is a criterion, not a forecast.

Screens: preparation, planning, recipe, library. Source: Owner, 2026-10-25 (#176 thread). Kind: OD.

**L146.** Each fermentation step says how to decide that it is done, with cues that fit what has happened to the dough so far:

- alongside the estimated range (L145), the step names the readiness cues to watch for, not only a time;
- a volume cue such as "about doubled" applies only when the dough has not been degassed since the step started. It does not apply after folds, a pre-shape or reshaping;
- after any degassing, the step offers cues that remain valid: a domed or slightly rounded surface, bubbles on the surface and sides, jiggle, poke test and feel;
- the cues come from the step's position in the process and the operations recorded so far, so they change if the baker adds or skips a fold;
- transitional steps with a fixed duration (for example 1 h on the counter, then 24 h in the fridge) stay fixed. Their readiness cues and time range are optional: available on demand or enabled per recipe, never prompted or shown as due. The step that follows the model (`durationMode: 'model'` in process.md, usually the final proof) absorbs the variation. On that step, the cues and the range are shown by default (L145);
- when the baker moves on to the next step, the engine recomputes every following step from the actual times and conditions recorded so far: the real fermentation already done, actual temperatures and container changes. It never keeps the original plan. The new estimates and ranges replace the old ones at once, and reminders follow.

Screens: preparation, planning, recipe. Source: Owner, 2026-10-25 (#176 thread). Kind: OD.
