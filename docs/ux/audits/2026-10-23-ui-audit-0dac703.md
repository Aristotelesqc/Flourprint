# Flourprint 0.3 mockup, independent UX/UI audit of revision 0dac703

**Target:** #133 mockup at `0dac7038fb5bea2892fa09f822c0932b861de9db` (build label `v0.3.0 · sturdy-meadow · local`). The hosted page was unreachable through the proxy (403 on CONNECT), so it was built from a read-only worktree (`pnpm build` and `pnpm build:mockups`) and served locally. `review-build.json` records the same revision.
**Date:** 2026-10-23. **Setup:** Playwright with the pre-installed Chromium, 393x852, DPR 2.75, touch, French first, dark first. Also light, AMOLED, 360 px at 130 % and EN text checks.
**Independence:** the gallery companions, PR text, motion clips and builder reports were not opened. The only prior material read was the earlier audit report supplied by the caller (of revision 0d63eb4).
**Files:** screenshots, scripts and extracted text: not committed.

**Counts:** 0 blocker, 5 major, 22 minor, 6 polish.

**Verdict:** much better than 0d63eb4. The 360 px / 130 % bar blocker is fixed, the carousel commit now animates, step drop is accepted in the drawn slot and refusals now say why. Relative times replace fixture dates, library search covers pre-dough names, and Preparation has a real journal, pause and retractable steps. It is not yet ready for owner acceptance: the Edit-mode per-stage ingredient rows are visibly broken at phone width, the primary Preparation button silently does nothing before the oven is marked ready, the ⋯ finish path proposes "Cuit" for a bake that never reached the oven, and several numbers still disagree (L118).

## 4. What works well (keep)

- Bottom bar at 360/130, all actions reachable and 48 px (Annuler, undo, redo, Enregistrer, ⋯).
- Carousel: 1:1 finger follow, animated commit and springback, instant change under reduced motion.
- Step drag: 350 ms hold, accepted slot matches the drawn slot, move preview sheet, visible refusal reason.
- Relative times everywhere before Planning sets a start.
- Hydration detail sheet reconciles to the gram (43 + 138,8 + 0,5 + 9,1 + 38 + 0,5 = 229,9) and the "≥" partial sums stay honest.
- Preparation: large countdown, ±15 min, working Pause/Resume (frozen display while held), ← Précédent retracts a completion, visible journal that matches the real clock, outcome and abandon confirmations as centred dialogs rounded on all corners.
- Tabs and segmented control keep their width when the bold state moves (no neighbour shift).
- Contrast: no body text below 4.5:1 in light, dark or AMOLED on Recipe, Edit, Planning, Preparation and Library (the primary-button flags in `t26.js` are a measurement artefact; the buttons were read visually and are fine).
- FR copy: no tu/vous found, no English leaks in FR and no French leaks in EN across the scanned screens.
- Library search covers pre-dough names; "Créer « Shokupan »" explicit.

## 5. Needs physical-device testing

Swipe feel and commit thresholds, 350 ms hold and edge autoscroll, soft keyboard over the Edit fields, native date picker format, Android Back during drag and sheets, haptics, system text scale (versus the 130 % emulation), real performance of the Preparation countdown, notification of preheat or step end.
