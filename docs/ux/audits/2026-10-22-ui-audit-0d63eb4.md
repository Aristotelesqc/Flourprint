# Flourprint 0.3 interactive mockup — independent UX/UI audit

Target: review `review-2026-10-21-compact-drag` at 0d63eb4 (same build as gh-pages, served locally because github.io/pages.dev were blocked from the sandbox). Phone emulation 393×852, DPR 2.75, touch, French, dark (`capture=1`); also light, AMOLED, 360 px / 130 % text, English scan. Date 2026-10-22.
Screenshots, scripts and raw measurements: not committed.

**Counts:** 1 blocker · 9 major · 31 minor · 8 polish. Owner points: 11 reproduced, 3 partly (1, 10, 13), none not reproduced.

**Verdict:** visual identity, accessibility plumbing and honest "≥" partial sums are solid. Recipe follows the engine's objects, not the baker's tasks; numbers are rounded inconsistently and disagree across views; the tab swipe skips its commit animation; step drop is flaky near a card edge; at 360 px / 130 % the bar actions are lost.

## Owner's 14 points

1. **Division before Pointage refused — partly.** A 350 ms hold drag dropped lower opens "Aperçu du déplacement"; "Appliquer" gives Pétrissage · Division · Pointage (screenshot not committed). Releasing in the slot visibly opened just above Pointage always cancels (y = 261 with list scrolled to 380: 2/2 cancelled; y ≥ 270: 6/6 worked) — drop hit-test and drawn slot disagree. Only feedback "Déplacement annulé", no reason; refusal reasons exist only in a screen-reader live region.
2. **Carousel snaps — reproduced (measured).** Track follows the finger 1:1, but on a committing release the next frame is already final (-60/-120/-230/-100 → -393, zero intermediate frames; release frame 56–85 ms). Non-committing springback is animated (133–200 ms). Likely cause: the section change runs before the slide animation, so the track re-renders at its final translateX. Velocity only sets duration (100–220 ms) with M3 standard easing from zero speed; a spring seeded with release velocity would carry the motion.
3. **"Quitter le brouillon ?" — reproduced.** "Rester ici" full-width neutral in body (327×48); "Abandonner" right-aligned outlined danger in a footer under a divider (108×48). Close × is also named "Rester ici". `.modal-sheet` radius 16 px but bottom corners square (footer paints over). Centred mid-screen (y 295–556) but styled like a bottom sheet (screenshot not committed). Good: native modal `<dialog>`, Back raises the same guard.
4. **Bold active tab shifts neighbours — reproduced.** Active 700 vs inactive 600 at 14 px; labels grow 1.2–1.5 px (Planification 84.2 → 85.7); neighbours move up to 1.6 px (Historique x 300.5 → 298.9). Same component in the Étapes groupées / Ligne de temps switch.
5. **Hydration chip — reproduced.** View: ⓘ centre 9.8 px off value centre (291.1 vs 281.3); edit: lock and ⓘ 9.8 px apart. Input 80×36 (< 48). "≥" lost in edit ("73"). Error message wraps into an 80 px column of 4 lines, label drops ~37 px. Card padding 10 px (siblings 12, DESIGN.md 16).
6. **"Sommes partielles…" — reproduced.** Plain span, not tappable; doesn't say hydration/fat/sugars are lower bounds; doesn't lead to the fix (Composition accordion at the bottom of edit mode).
7. **"Ingrédients par étape · Pesée" — reproduced.** Subtitle "% du boulanger" is a column legend; a stray copy ends the page (screenshot not committed). Grams 13.5 px off the name baseline. Tangzhong header "4,5 % de la farine totale · 31,8 % du poids de farine totale" ambiguous. Tangzhong hydration 580,5 % disabled. Counter global only (0/11 → 2/11), no per-group count even collapsed (`32-…`, `33-…`). Group 2 subtitle repeats its title.
8. **Edit focus highlight — reproduced.** `.ingredient-row.is-editing` padding `8px 0`, radius 0; highlight starts at content x (screenshot not committed).
9. **Baker's % twice, different rounding — reproduced.** Lait "36 %" (share in final additions) beside "49,68 %" field (recipe total); Lait en poudre 4,5 vs 4,46; Sucre 15,9 vs 15,92; tangzhong 4,5 vs 4,46; yeast 2,9 % / 2,87 / 2,866 %.
10. **Delete ingredients — partly.** "Retirer" exists only in the 6th accordion at the bottom of edit mode, not on the row; all buttons named just "Retirer"; confirmation repeats itself; flour and tangzhong ingredients cannot be removed.
11. **Unexplained accordions — reproduced.** Six closed accordions after the list, no subtitles: Taille de la recette, Totaux, Ajouter un composant, Ajouter un ingrédient, Composition, Ingrédients. Taille repeats its title; Totaux duplicates the rows' editable "Total recette" (two edit paths); Composition buttons read as status.
12. **Steps a separate tab — reproduced** (consistent with L49/L70 as currently written: an owner requirements decision, not a bug).
13. **Planning editable — partly.** No inline editors or handles, but each of 10 rows has a pencil that silently creates a draft from view mode ("Prêtes" → "Brouillon"), jumps to Étapes and opens the step editor (`40-…`). In edit mode the fermentation panel offers "Appliquer au brouillon". Planning repeats Steps 1:1; the bar's calendar button duplicates the Planification tab.
14. **Summary icons — reproduced.** Flour bag with +, balance, drop, battery-like bar (fat), cube (sugars); only the drop reads.

## What works well (keep)

- Native modal `<dialog>`, focus return, Escape; disclosures expose `aria-expanded`.
- Handles and checkboxes 48×48 with accessible names; row tap toggles once.
- Contrast passes in dark, light, AMOLED; AMOLED true black.
- French: no tu/vous; infinitive instructions.
- Honest "≥" partial sums; hydration sheet reconciles to the gram.
- Move preview applies atomically and announces; "Monter"/"Descendre" non-drag alternative.
- Carousel 1:1 finger-follow; animated springback.
- Draft guard on Annuler and Back.
- Template chooser with explicit "Créer « … »"; search with empty state.
- Preparation layout: big remaining time, step count, quick controls.

## Needs physical-device testing

Swipe feel and thresholds (0.3 px/ms, 20 px) and the 56–85 ms release frame; 350 ms hold and edge autoscroll; Android Back during drag/modal; soft keyboard; native date picker (headless showed US format); haptics; system 130 % text scale.

## Not checked

Reduced motion; full keyboard traversal; all 15 recipes in edit mode; English beyond a text scan; light/AMOLED at 360 px; component catalogue.
