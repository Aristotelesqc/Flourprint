# Belz 2017: salt series (rheofermentometer)

Data file: `belz-2017-salt.csv` (6 rows, one per salt level), from Tables 1 and 2 of the paper. Extracted by script from the full-text XML on 2026-11-07. The paper's yeast series (Table 1, right-hand columns) has no gas measurement and is not kept, nor are the significance letters.

Use: at 1.72 % of flour total gas is 0.71 of the salt-free value but retained gas 0.93; at 2.87 %, 0.44 and 0.66 (our division). The salt-free dough loses 36 % of its gas, against under 1 % in Boudrag 2026. One flour, one yeast, one temperature, a fixed end time: gas at a fixed time, not a rate law.
