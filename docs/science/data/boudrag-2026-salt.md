Data file: `boudrag-2026-salt.csv` (12 rows: white, wholemeal and fibre-fortified flour x 4 NaCl levels). Only four columns of the original tables are kept: total CO2 `Vt`, retained CO2 `Vr`, retention ratio `R` (`Vr / Vt`, in %) and maximum dough height `Hm`. Values are mean and standard deviation as printed in the Gemini report.
