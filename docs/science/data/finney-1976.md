## Rows

- **Bulk fermentation optimum** (p. 130, Fig. 3, plus p. 126-128): 2 % for 180 min, 3.5 % for 120 min, 7.2 % for 70 min, 12 % for 45 min. At 70 min, above 7.2 % the doughs became "progressively stiff and bucky" and the breads looked overdeveloped, below 7.2 % they were underdeveloped; the 12 % bread at 45 min had a slightly brownish, noticeably wet crumb and a bitter or yeast-like flavour. Fermentation times "appreciably less than 70 min require undesirably large amounts of yeast".
- **Optimum proof time** (p. 127, 128, 132, Fig. 6): 55 min after 180 min, 36.5 after 120, 21.5 after 70, 12 after 45 ("rather than 13.75 min as the broken part of the regression line suggests").
- **Equal gas** (p. 130, Fig. 5): gas production at 30 °C on 10 g flour reaches the same level, "about 350 gassing units", at 188, 78 and 53 min for 2, 7.2 and 12 %. These are the bulk times plus 8 min, "fermentation takes place before ingredients are added and dough mixing ends".
- **Equal total gas** (p. 133, Fig. 7): the total fermentation times, bulk + proof + 16 min (8 min before the end of mixing and 8 min of inactivation after the loaf enters the oven), are 251, 107.5 and 73 min for 2, 7.2 and 12 %. The authors conclude that total gas production is constant for any balanced, optimised system.

## What it shows and does not show

- It is an independent measured dose response, at least in the sense that its observable (optimum loaf, equal gas) is not Craig's table. Three of its four doses (3.5, 7.2, 12 %) are above the shipped 3 % gate; its dough has 6 % sucrose (above the 5 % gate for added sugar), 3 % shortening (at the 3 % limit) and 4 % milk solids; its endpoints are loaf-quality optima, not a dough-ready or height endpoint. It supports the **order of magnitude** of the dose exponent (0.68 to 0.83 depending on the observable). It is not a validation of the shipped 0.729 at home doses.
- The 2 % gas curve of Fig. 5 starts slowly and bends upward over its first hour or two, while the 7.2 and 12 % curves are steeper from the start (observation from the figure, no fit; the figure carries no digitised values here).
- The six-row table (2.0, 2.5, 3.5, 5.3, 7.2, 8.6 %) is **not** in this 1976 paper. Its yeast, fermentation and punch and pan times are printed as Table V (p. 26) of Finney 1984, read in full on 2026-11-11 ([finney-1984.md](finney-1984.md)), a prescribed schedule of a later method paper, shown beside and never in place of these experimental pairs. That table has no proof column; its proof times as quoted by an external review (for example 27 min at 7.2 %) are not printed in the 1984 paper either, and differ from the 21.5 min of this paper. AACC Method 10-10.03 itself was not read.
